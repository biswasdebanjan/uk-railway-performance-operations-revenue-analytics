# Analytical Methodology

## Analytical spine

**Network Performance → Delay Diagnostics → Customer & Booking Behaviour → Revenue Impact → Executive Action**

The analysis moves from network-level performance into operational diagnosis, customer and booking behaviour, commercial impact and prioritised action.

## Method

The analytical frameworks were developed using:

- histogram distribution analysis
- percentile-based evaluation
- statistical reasoning
- business rule validation
- operational interpretation

The purpose was to create analytical segments that were both data-informed and meaningful for railway performance decisions.
